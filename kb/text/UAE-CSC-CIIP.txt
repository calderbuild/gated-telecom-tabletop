Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Policy
The Critical Information Infrastructure Protection (CIIP) Policydefines the governance and protection framework for the UAE’s CII entities. It establishes a unified approach to identifying critical assets, developing national risk profiles, setting baseline security requirements, and implementing assurance and enforcement mechanisms across vital sectors.
The Cyber Security Council has established this policy to ensure a baseline measure of security and cyber resilience of its Critical Information Infrastructure (CII), aligned with the UAE’s national priority to be a global leader in cyber security, and to implement measures towards a resilient and secure cyberspace for its CII.
